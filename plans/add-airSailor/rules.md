# Rules brief: Add Air Sailor discipline (add-airSailor)

All entries below are summarised from RULES-FAQ **Q042** (full talent texts live there). DISC = text-discipline-players.txt, TAL = text-talents-players.txt, PG = text-RB-players-guide.txt (all under `rulebook extracts/rulebook extracts/`).

## R1 — Discipline header
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** Important attributes Charisma, Dexterity, Willpower. Artisan skills Rune Carving, Sail Embroidery. Durability 5. Free talent at Circle 1: Air Sailing. Karma ritual: silent meditation under the open sky picturing an airship journey; complete when the imagined journey ends safely (half an hour). Half-Magic: airship designs/types, flight paths, crew complements, maintenance/repair/construction.
- **Sources:** Q042; DISC:1-14; PG:2978-3024 (p.88-89)
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T1

## R2 — Per-Circle table (C1-8)
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** C1 Avoid Blow, Climbing, Melee Weapons, Thread Weaving (Air Weaving), Wind Catcher; karma once/round on any action while aboard an airship. C2 +1 Physical Defense; Awareness. C3 karma on Initiative; Empathic Sense. C4 +1 Social Defense; Wound Balance. C5 Collaborate (Simple, 1 Strain, ally +2 to a test toward a common goal) + karma on Interaction tests; Heartening Laugh. C6 +2 Physical Defense; Air Dance. C7 additional Recovery test; Inspire Others. C8 +3 Physical Defense; Lion Heart.
- **Sources:** Q042; DISC:10-35; PG:3018-3051 (p.89)
- **Decision:** Book is silent on whether the +1/+2/+3 Physical Defense bonuses stack (Archer same); follow the existing repo convention (`stacking: highest`, replace). Resolved 2026-11-22, no new owner ruling needed.
- **Used by:** T1

## R5 — Tier
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** The book prints no tier on talents (Q001: tier follows the Circle learned). Skill-use tier is as printed per talent (oddity: Engaging Banter, Etiquette, Graceful Exit, Surprise Strike print Novice skill use but are Journeyman options).
- **Sources:** Q042, Q001
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T2, T3

## R6 — Thread Weaving (Air Weaving) and non-casting
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** Thread Weaving sets max permanent threads and max thread rank (= talent Rank) and grants Thread Sight; Air Sailors have no Spellcasting/spells/matrix. App keeps the name `Thread Weaving (Air Sailor)` (the engine looks up `Thread Weaving (<discipline>)`).
- **Sources:** Q042; TAL:656-659; PG:6256-6303, 8083-8126, 2186-2189
- **Decision:** Owner 2026-11-22: no spells; thread weaving is for karma/threads to magic items.
- **Used by:** T1, T2

## R8 — Creation / crew requirement
- **Status:** ANSWERED (crew rule) / NOT-COVERED (starting kit)
- **Ruling:** No Air Sailor-specific kit; the generic creation kit applies. Joining an airship crew needs Air Sailing Rank 1+, met automatically by the free talent.
- **Sources:** Q042; TAL:14; PG:4288-4289
- **Decision:** Generic kit (non-magician) applies; not a house rule.
- **Used by:** T4
