# Rules brief: custom-item-builder (custom-item-builder)

## R1 — Unarmed damage = Strength step only; weapons add their Damage Step to Strength
- **Status:** FAQ-HIT
- **Ruling:** Unarmed Combat damage is the Strength step with no base fist step (effectively +0). Weapons add Damage Step to Strength.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q025 (PG p.177, 378-379)
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T2

## R2 — Unarmed attacks are close combat; no range increments
- **Status:** FAQ-HIT
- **Ruling:** Unarmed attacks are close combat vs Physical Defense, reach ~2 yards; no short/long range stated.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q025 (PG p.391-392)
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T4

## R3 — Attached weapon (gauntlet) Damage Step vs Strength
- **Status:** NOT-COVERED
- **Ruling:** Books say attached weapons use Unarmed Combat (PG p.406) but not how their Damage Step combines with Strength.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q026; text-RB-players-guide.txt:15328-15333
- **Decision:** Owner 2026-10-05: additive house convention (Strength step + item Damage Step), same as ordinary weapons.
- **Used by:** T1, T2

## R4 — Weapon Damage Step of 0
- **Status:** NOT-COVERED
- **Ruling:** Books silent on validity of 0.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q026
- **Decision:** Owner 2026-10-05: accept and store 0 as an app convention.
- **Used by:** T3
