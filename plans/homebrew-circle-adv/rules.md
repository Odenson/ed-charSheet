# Rules brief: Homebrew circle advancement (homebrew-circle-adv)

Source: PG p.453 "Optional Rule", text-RB-players-guide.txt:17139-17176 (extracts nested at `rulebook extracts/rulebook extracts/`; FAQ Q007 line numbers are stale). FAQ: Q007, Q029, Q030.

## R1 — Optional advancement gate and table
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** Instead of all lower-Circle Discipline Talents at rank = target Circle, a character knows N talents available to his Discipline (Discipline Talents OR Talent Options) at a minimum rank, and one of them must be from his current Circle. Table (new Circle: min # talents / min rank / single talent from Circle):
  2: 5/2/1 · 3: 6/3/2 · 4: 7/4/3 · 5: 8/5/4 · 6: 9/6/5 · 7: 10/7/6 · 8: 11/8/7 · 9: 12/9/8 · 10: 13/10/9 · 11: 14/11/10 · 12: 15/11/11 · 13: 16/12/12 · 14: 17/12/13 · 15: 18/13/14.
  The table is keyed by new Circle only — NOT by number of Disciplines.
- **Sources:** PG:17139-17176 (p.453); FAQ Q029
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** all

## R2 — What the optional rule replaces
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** Replaces only the Minimum Talent Requirements gate (p.452; Talent Options normally play no role, here they count). Training with a higher-Circle member, 40 hours/3 weeks, fees, buying the new Discipline Talent + one Talent Option all unchanged. Versatility-learned talents and thread-rank bonuses never count toward advancement.
- **Sources:** PG:17127-17137, 17185-17234, 6410-6415, 9631-9636
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** all

## R3 — Legend Awards guidance for Circles 11-15
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** Award half the GM-guide recommended Legend in Circles 11-12, one third in 13-15. GM guidance only; no app effect.
- **Sources:** PG:17152-17156
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** none (informational)

## R4 — Multiple Disciplines
- **Status:** NOT-COVERED (owner decided)
- **Ruling:** Book is silent on how a multi-Discipline character is evaluated. Shared Discipline Talents count for all applicable Disciplines (PG:17345-17350).
- **Decision:** 2026-11-13 owner: evaluate per Discipline, counting only that Discipline's own talents (Discipline Talents + its Talent Options); the single talent must come from that Discipline's current Circle.
- **Used by:** all

## R5 — Free talents under the optional rule
- **Status:** ANSWERED (inference from silence + a general rule)
- **Ruling:** Free talents do not count toward N (free-talent rule: "does not count for the purposes of qualifying for the next Circle"). Durability and Karma Ritual are not talents. Only Discipline Talents + Talent Options count. Mica example (Swordmaster to Circle 3, 6 talents at rank 3) is consistent.
- **Sources:** text-RB-players-guide.txt:2905-2911, 17139-17144, 17131-17137; FAQ Q030
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T2

## R6 — "One from his current Circle"
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** The talent must have been gained at exactly the Circle being left (table column = new Circle − 1). A Talent Option taken at that Circle counts. Not covered: Circle of a later-learned option (Specific Training/Versatility); natural reading is the Circle of the slot taken.
- **Sources:** text-RB-players-guide.txt:17147-17148, 17157-17176, 17203-17208; FAQ Q030
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T2
