# Rules brief: Edit Gahad trigger (edit-gahad-trigger)

## R1 — What Gahad / a gahad trigger is
- **Status:** ANSWERED
- **Ruling:** Largely roleplaying. Triggers are an optional-rules construct: the player describes "a couple of impulses" in short sentences, optionally with limits/exceptions. Giving in or failing a Willpower (6) resist gives +1 to Action/Effect tests toward ending the stimulus or revenge; GM adjudicates all use.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q032; text-RB-players-guide.txt:1494-1537 (pp. 49-50); QAs_Races.txt:176-210
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T2, T3

## R2 — Number of triggers; who sets/changes them
- **Status:** NOT-COVERED (changing); ANSWERED (count/author)
- **Ruling:** Book: several triggers ("a couple"), player authors them, GM adjudicates. Silent on changing them later.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q032; text-RB-players-guide.txt:1506-1513
- **Decision:** 2026-11-15 owner: no house rule needed; the trigger may be edited at any time, under the user's control. Feature scope is deliberately a single trigger (simplification, not a rule).
- **Used by:** T2, T3
