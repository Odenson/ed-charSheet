# Q&A log: spell-extra-weave-and-extra-cast

## 2026-10-02 — asked by interrogator
**Q:** Where do you see only one of the two effects (extra-thread/weave pick vs extra-success/cast) applied or recorded? Options: Effect roll step bonus; Active effects after self-cast; Target effects readout on a cast on Other; cast success banner; other.
**A:** Casting the spell Pain: roll an extra weave, on success select the extra thread, which assigns an increased effect plus one wound. Then cast the spell and get extra successes. The resulting UI only shows the last cast success ("active increased duration plus two rounds"). In Target effects, with two successes vs the target number, the effects are lost: it only states a successful hit, "2 successes vs target number", but no effects, and the duration is unchanged.

## 2026-10-02 — asked by interrogator
**Q:** What should Target effects show for Pain with the +1 Wound pick and 2 successes: itemised lines with computed duration, itemised with duration text, or other?
**A:** Cast on other: Target effects should list the increased effect (+1 Wound, or whichever extra-thread option was selected) and also the increased duration (+2 rounds). Cast on this character: the extra duration should be an effect on the character, along with any other effect an extra success brings.

## 2026-10-02 — asked by interrogator
**Q:** Target effects duration format: computed rounds (Rank + boost) or base text + boost?
**A:** (a) Computed rounds, e.g. "Duration 7 rounds (Rank 5 + 2)", falling back to text when not round-countable.

## 2026-10-02 — asked by interrogator
**Q:** Self-cast Active effects row: sub-line chips for all applied options, only unfolded ones, or leave alone?
**A:** (a) Sub-line chips for all applied options (extra-thread picks + success-level option with multiplier); countdown keeps boosted rounds.

## 2026-10-02 — asked by interrogator
**Q:** Anything else material, or is that everything?
**A:** That's everything.
