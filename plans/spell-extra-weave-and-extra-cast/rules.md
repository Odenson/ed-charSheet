# Rules brief: spell-extra-weave-and-extra-cast (spell-extra-weave-and-extra-cast)

## R1 — Success levels and the Success Levels option
- **Status:** FAQ-HIT
- **Ruling:** One success at the Difficulty Number, +1 per full 5 over. The spell's Success Levels option applies once per ADDITIONAL success (levels − 1); e.g. +2 rounds with two extra successes = +4 rounds.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q012; manual/text-player-guide-game-concepts.txt:107–115 (p.34); text-player-guide-spell-concepts.txt:977–983 (p.270)
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T1, T2

## R2 — Extra Thread options stack and combine
- **Status:** ANSWERED (APP-DIFFERS on flow)
- **Ruling:** Each additional thread has its own effect; the same option may repeat and distinct options combine. Cap by Circle (1-4:1, 5-8:2, 9-12:3, 13-15:4). Book declares threads up front; app picks per successful extra weave.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q016; text-player-guide-spell-concepts.txt:392–415, 984–1004 (pp.256–257, 270–271)
- **Decision:** Owner 2026-10-02: keep the app's weave-then-pick flow.
- **Used by:** T1, T2

## R3 — "Rank" in durations and additive duration boosts
- **Status:** FAQ-HIT
- **Ruling:** Rank = caster's Spellcasting rank; duration boosts from Success Levels and Extra Threads add to the base.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q015; text-player-guide-spell-concepts.txt:447–454, 924–929, 995–998
- **Decision:** none
- **Used by:** T1

## R4 — Extra Thread and Success Level effects both apply to one cast
- **Status:** ANSWERED (inference from absence of restriction)
- **Ruling:** Threads fixed before the cast, success levels from the cast result; they combine, and duration options from each add.
- **Sources:** RULES-FAQ Q016; text-player-guide-spell-concepts.txt:977–989 (p.270)
- **Decision:** Owner stated both must apply (2026-10-02 interview).
- **Used by:** T1, T2
